Bio
Full artist story, education and creative identity.

Alireza, professionally known as ALISMI, is a multi‑genre artist whose journey began at just 12 years old. What started as curiosity quickly became purpose. At 15, he picked up the guitar for the first time and discovered the emotional depth of music. At 18, a keyboard gifted by his father reignited everything, pushing him into production, composition, and the world he was meant for.

He immersed himself in rap culture, developing his skills as a rapper, composer, producer, mixing engineer, and mastering engineer. Every track became a piece of his identity built from scratch, shaped with precision, and driven by raw emotion.

After losing his mother, ALISMI moved to England alone, rebuilding his life from zero with nothing but ambition and a vision. Now based in London, he dedicates up to 15 hours a day to creating producing beats, writing lyrics, studying sound, and refining his craft with relentless discipline.

He earned his Diploma and Higher National Certificate in Music Production & Performing Arts from UAL (University of the Arts London), solidifying his technical foundation and artistic direction. Today, he continues expanding his creative world by studying Media, adding visual storytelling, digital creation, and modern communication to his skillset.

ALISMI blends Trap, EDM, Afro, R&B, and Reggaeton into a unique sonic identity. His music is built from experience, ambition, and evolution. This isn’t just noise — it’s ALISMI.
